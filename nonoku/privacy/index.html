Nonoku: privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

No account, no sign-up and no server of our own. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Only the ad network and the app store receive the data they need to show ads and process purchases.

Data on your device

Puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, hints, settings and the items you own. Android may include them in your device backup if backup is on; we never receive this data.

Advertising

The Google Play version shows ads through Google AdMob (Google). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, it may process the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it later in Settings. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Details: Google privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps.

Other versions show no ads.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play and Huawei AppGallery under their own terms. We only receive a confirmation of which item you own and never see your payment details.

Children

Nonoku is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device. We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to export or erase. Questions about advertising data go to the ad network, questions about purchases go to the app store.

Security

The ad and store SDKs send data encrypted (TLS).

Changes

When something changes, we update this page and its effective date.
