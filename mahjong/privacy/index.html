Mahjong Solitaire: privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

No account and no sign-up. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Only the ad network, the app store, Google Play Games and our device-check service receive the data they need to show ads, process purchases, save achievements and protect rewards from cheating.

Data on your device

Game progress, statistics, streak, hints, settings and the items you own. The wallet and purchases are also kept as a copy sealed with a hardware key of the device; that copy never leaves the device and is excluded from Android backup. Android may include progress and settings in your device backup if backup is on; we never receive this data.

Progress file

"Save progress to file" in Settings creates a file only when you choose it, and stores it where you choose. The file holds progress, statistics, streak and settings, never purchases or payment data. Loading a file replaces these on the phone.

Device check (Google Play version only)

To protect purchases and rewards from cheating, the app checks that the device and the app are genuine. It asks Google Play Integrity for a verdict and sends a hardware key attestation (a certificate chain that describes the security state of the device: boot state, security patch level, the package name of the app and its signing certificate) together with the Play Integrity token to our verification service, hosted on Cloudflare. No account, name, advertising ID or game data is sent. The service keeps no data and writes no request data to logs; Cloudflare processes the IP address to deliver the request. Details: Google privacy policy, Cloudflare privacy policy. If the check cannot run (offline, no Google Play services), the game works normally.

Google Play Games (Google Play version only)

The app signs in automatically with your Play Games profile if you use one, and saves your achievements to it; Google processes this under its privacy policy. You can turn Play Games off in the Play Games app. There are no cloud saves.

Advertising

The Google Play version shows ads through Google AdMob (Google). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, it may process the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it later in Settings. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Details: Google privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps.

Other versions show no ads.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play and Huawei AppGallery under their own terms. We only receive a confirmation of which item you own and never see your payment details.

Children

Mahjong Solitaire is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device. We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to export or erase. Questions about advertising data go to the ad network, questions about purchases go to the app store.

Security

The ad, store, Play Games and verification connections are encrypted (TLS).

Changes

When something changes, we update this page and its effective date.
